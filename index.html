The Problem

The industry runs on copies of the truth.

The same trade is captured eight, ten, fifteen times — in different systems, in different dialects, governed by different teams. The copies drift apart. Then people reconcile them back together. Reconciliation isn't a workflow problem; it's a structural condition of how finance stores truth.

⧉

Duplicate data

One economic event, recorded over and over across siloed platforms.

⚖

Multiple truths

Front, middle, and back office disagree on position, risk, and P&L.

↻

Reconciliation overhead

Teams and tools whose entire job is arguing copies back into agreement.

⚠

Reactive reporting

Regulatory output assembled after the fact — brittle, manual, error-prone.

The Shift

Stop fixing breaks downstream.
Prevent them upstream.

FIOS stores only the validated Financial Event and the reference entities it touches. Everything else — position, ledger, risk, settlement instruction, every regulatory report — is derived from it, on demand, never copied.
